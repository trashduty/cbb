CBB Opening Results

Frozen opening snapshots. Positive-edge model selections. Actual final scores.

How this record works. Each selection is frozen at its first evaluable pregame Best Book quote, selected by highest calculated edge across the tracked sportsbooks, using the model and price available at that capture. Only an edge strictly above zero enters the ledger. These are hypothetical model selections, not a record of bets placed or official picks. No historic opening record has been reconstructed.

Definitions, Timing, And Grading

Opening means first observed by this tracker after it was enabled, not necessarily the sportsbook’s first published line. Each spread side, moneyline side, Over, and Under is captured once per event. At the first capture with a valid model probability, the highest-edge available book is selected; later books and quotes do not replace it. Earlier DraftKings-only snapshots remain unchanged; see Book and Snapshot Policy in the ledger. Later line changes, price changes, or model updates do not rewrite that snapshot or add a second bet for the same selection.

Opening edge is the snapshot model probability minus the break-even probability implied by its captured sportsbook price. Spread and total probabilities are evaluated at that captured line. Best Book and consensus-market edges from the live board are separate analyses and are not mixed into this opening record.

Missing model data: new Best Book captures wait for an evaluable pregame quote and model probability. Once frozen, the record is not filled from later predictions. Older excluded DraftKings snapshots remain excluded.

Consensus shows whether all three models favored the recorded spread, Over, or Under at its captured line. Yes includes qualifying selections from either market. A tie or disagreement yields No. Older totals can use verified model data from their original capture commit. Not Recorded means the original consensus could not be verified.

Default view: All Graded Bets shows wins, losses, and pushes across the selected bet types. Choose All Bets (Graded + Pending) or Pending in the Result filter to inspect ungraded selections.

Tiers: 0–0.9% means greater than 0% and less than 1%; 1–2.9% means at least 1% and less than 3%; 3–5.9% means at least 3% and less than 6%; 6%+ means at least 6%. Boundaries use the unrounded edge, so the ranges have no gaps.

Results: only scores marked completed by The Odds API are graded. Spread result uses the selected team’s final margin plus its frozen handicap; totals use combined final points against the frozen total; moneyline uses the outright winner. Exact ties with a spread or total are pushes. Overtime is included in final scores. Unfinished, canceled, postponed, or unmatched games stay pending until a valid final is available; pending does not mean a loss.

Net units: one unit is risked on every ledger selection. Wins pay the actual captured American odds, losses lose one unit, and pushes return the stake. This is hypothetical flat-stake performance, not a promised return. An Over/Under is counted once per event and side, not once per team row.

Updates: snapshots are captured after successful scraper runs. A separate grading workflow checks scores every 30 minutes even if a scraper fails. Recent score availability is limited; unresolved older games remain pending for review or a score import. This page checks published data every minute. Dates use Eastern Time; capture and grading timestamps display in UTC.
